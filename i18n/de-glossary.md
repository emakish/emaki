# German glossary

Use natural German with formal Sie, ordinary German noun capitalization and short
infinitive button labels. Translate whole messages, preserving placeholders, markup,
line breaks and stable identifiers. German has two plural forms:
`nplurals=2; plural=(n != 1);`.

Keep Emaki, niri, Arch, AUR, Flathub, Secure Boot, GRUB and snapper unchanged.
Other product names, command names, paths, units and protocol identifiers remain
unchanged. Translate visible application names, never their stable keys. Preserve
Qt date-pattern field layout. Firmware menu names quoted as instructions remain
as shown in that firmware. A Sudo-Schlüssel is an authorization grant, not a saved
password; its duration in turns is expressed as Schritte and its turn reporting
as Schrittberichte.
