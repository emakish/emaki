# Brazilian Portuguese glossary

Use natural Brazilian Portuguese, sentence case, short button labels and você.
Preserve placeholders, markup, line breaks and stable identifiers. Product names
Emaki, niri, Arch, AUR, Flathub, Secure Boot, GRUB and snapper stay unchanged.
