# Latin American Spanish glossary

Use neutral Latin American Spanish, informal tú, sentence case and concise buttons.
Translate complete messages; preserve placeholders, markup, line breaks and identifiers.
Spanish has two plural forms: `nplurals=2; plural=(n != 1);`.
