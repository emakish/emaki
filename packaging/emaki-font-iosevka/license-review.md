Regular, Bold, Italic and BoldItalic. The archive SHA-256 is

The font family is Iosevka Nerd Font Mono, not the reserved Pomicons, Font Awesome,
Bitstream or Vera names. Weather Icons' upstream notice contains placeholder
reserved-name fields; preserve it verbatim rather than inventing a reservation.
OFL permits bundling with software with the notice retained, bars selling the font
alone, and requires preserving its licence and reserved-name restrictions.
MIT and Bitstream Vera require notices; Bitstream Vera also restricts names and
standalone font sales. CC-BY-4.0 requires attribution, its licence link and change
indication: the README identifies the upstream sets and Nerd Fonts release;
Nerd Fonts patched those glyphs into Iosevka. Emaki only selects four files.
Apache-2.0 requires its licence and applicable notices; retain the component notice
and full Apache text. No trademark permission for depicted brands is implied.
These component terms do not relicense the font under the repository's GPL.
